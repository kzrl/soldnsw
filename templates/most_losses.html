About this data: losses are nominal and based on recorded settlement history. Same-day resales are excluded. A loss is the latest recorded sale being below the immediately preceding recorded sale for the same property. Records are linked by property ID, or by identical address when IDs differ; changed property IDs, missing sales, partial titles, related-party transfers or data errors can affect results. Rows with a drop of more than 80% are flagged as possible data issues on the suburb losses page.
